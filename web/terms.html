2. What SPPS is, and what it is not

SPPS is software. It helps practitioners keep client records, run assessments over time, prepare reports, stay in touch with athletes between sessions and be found by people looking for support. It helps athletes and guardians find a practitioner, complete what their practitioner asks of them and control what is shared.

SPPS does not provide psychological, medical, psychiatric or any other professional service, diagnosis, treatment or advice. Every professional service is provided by the practitioner you choose, who is solely responsible for it. We do not employ, supervise or endorse practitioners, and a listing or a verified badge is not a recommendation. Any decision to work with a practitioner is yours.

SPPS never writes clinical narrative on anyone's behalf. Text in a note, formulation or report is written by the practitioner.

3. Not an emergency service

SPPS is not a crisis, emergency or round-the-clock service. Messages, check-ins, alerts and appointment requests are not monitored continuously and may not be read for days.
