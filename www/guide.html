How to use SPPS

SPPS Version 1.0 - Free · A step-by-step guide for practitioners, the platform owner, athletes, guardians, coaches and anyone looking for support. Some features appear only when the platform owner has switched them on; where that is the case it is marked when switched on.

1. Before you start

SPPS works in any up-to-date browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and in the SPPS Android app. Everything is saved on the server, so you can start on one device and continue on another; e-mailed links work in whichever browser or app you open them in. SPPS is not an emergency service: in an emergency call 112.

Use the theme buttons at the top (Original, Light, Dark) to choose the look you prefer.

2. Practitioners: joining SPPS

Step 1 — Register. Open Join free. Enter your name, e-mail address and a password, your qualifications, years of experience, achievements and specialisms, and add a profile photograph. Accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy and send the form.

Step 2 — Confirm your e-mail. Open the confirmation e-mail and press the link. It works in any browser, even a different one from where you registered: your application is already saved on the server.

Step 3 — Wait for review. The platform owner reviews every application. If more information is needed you will be told what is missing the next time you sign in, and you can answer from the same screen. You are told by e-mail when a decision is made.

Step 4 — Sign in. Open Practitioner login, enter your e-mail and password, tick the box to accept the terms and press Sign in securely. If you forget your password press Forgot Password? on the sign-in page.

3. Practitioners: the workspace

The menu on the left takes you to each area:

Overview — counts of athletes, active cases, new enquiries and open drafts, and the latest requests. Enquiries — service enquiries sent to you from the public pages; open one to reply, change its status or turn it into an athlete record. Appointments — publish free appointment slots and review requests. Athletes & Cases — your caseload (see section 4). Assessments & Trends — record assessment administrations and see longitudinal trends. Monitoring — record monitoring scores against factors you define. Load & Recovery — load and recovery indices calculated from your entries. Injury Psychology — injury episodes and psychological check-ins during return to sport. Interventions — your intervention library and assignments. Reports — report drafts. Settings (Profile & Privacy) — your profile, directory profile, templates, data download and account controls.

Use Refresh to check for new requests, and Logout when you finish, especially on a shared device.

4. Practitioners: athlete records

Add an athlete. Press + New athlete, give a code and a name, and confirm that service consent has been recorded. To add many athletes at once, use Import from a spreadsheet in Settings: upload a .csv or .xlsx file, match the columns, check every row in the preview, then import. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Open a record. Choose the athlete in Athletes & Cases. The buttons on the record open:

Profile — age group, level, guardian details for under-18s, referral source and goals. Timeline — everything recorded for the athlete, newest first. Formulation (5Ps) — presenting, predisposing, precipitating, perpetuating and protective factors; each save keeps the earlier version. Write note — a consultation note (see below). Assessments over time — enter scores and see trends (see below). Reports to share — reports for a chosen audience (section 5). Athlete app — invite the athlete or guardian (section 6). Access log — who opened or changed the record and when; never what it says.

Cases. Open a case with + New case once consent is recorded. Cases hold notes, monitoring entries and intervention assignments.

Consultation notes. Choose the case and date, optionally a note template, and type. Your draft is saved to the server as you type, and if you lose connection it is kept encrypted on your device and sent when you are back online. When the note is complete press Sign and lock; a signed note cannot be changed, but you can add a dated amendment. Create your own note templates in Settings.

Assessments over time. Choose an instrument and press Enter scores. Published tests are entered as subscale scores only (you must hold the licence; never type the test's items). Your own scales, built with New scale, can be answered item by item. Leave anything not scored empty: it is shown as missing, never estimated. Press Trend to see each subscale over time.

5. Practitioners: reports and coach links

Templates. In Settings, create a report template with New report template. Each field is tagged with the audiences that may see it (athlete, coach, medical); a field with no audience is clinical-only and is never shared.

Write and sign. In Reports to share, choose a template, an audience and a title. Write the narrative yourself; scores and charts are added from the athlete's assessments. Save drafts as you go, then press Sign report. A signed report cannot be changed.

Record consent. Under Consent to share, record who gave consent (athlete, or guardian for under-18s), for which audience, and how (written or verbal). Consent can be withdrawn later; links that rely on it stop working.

Share with a coach when switched on. Open the signed report and press Create share link. Copy the link at once — it is shown only once — and send it to the coach. Links expire, show every opening under the report, and can be revoked with Revoke. You can also download a PDF.

Ask the athlete to approve when switched on. For an athlete linked in the athlete app, open a signed coach report and press Ask the athlete to approve. The athlete (or guardian for under-18s) sees exactly what the coach would see and decides; nothing is sent unless they approve.

6. Practitioners: the athlete app when switched on

Invite. On the athlete's record press Athlete app, then send the invitation (for under-18s it goes to the guardian first, using the guardian e-mail in the profile). The invitation shows exactly what you will see. Nothing flows until it is accepted.

Once linked you can: set a check-in plan (daily or weekly items such as stress, mood and confidence); add alert rules (for example stress at or above 8) that flag a check-in for your attention; assign programmes made of tasks; and release short summaries the athlete (and, if you choose, the guardian) can read. Open alerts are shown on the record; press Acknowledge when you have dealt with one. Remember that SPPS does not notify anyone in real time.

End a link with End link. What was shared before stays in your record.

7. Practitioners: directory and enquiries when switched on

In Settings, fill in your Public directory profile (name shown, city, sports, athlete levels, age groups, languages, online or in person, whether you are taking new clients, qualifications and an “about” text) and press Submit for publication. The platform owner checks and publishes it. Saving changes later takes the listing offline until it is published again.

Enquiries from the directory appear under your directory profile. Reply directly to the person, then press Mark as replied. When self-serve accounts are switched on you can also press Accept and invite to create the athlete record and send the athlete-app invitation in one step (confirm first that you have agreed the service with the athlete or guardian), or Decline.

8. Practitioners: your data and your account

Download all your data as one ZIP file from Settings at any time (you are asked for your password). Your data explains who can see what you record. Close your account when switched on from Settings: confirm with your password, use the notice period to tell your clients and choose for each athlete record whether to export and erase it or keep deactivated, then press Complete closure. You can cancel during the notice period.

9. Platform owner

Sign in with the owner account to see the Admin Dashboard alongside your own practitioner workspace.

Registration requests. See every application with its status and history. Open one to read it, then Approve, Request more info (with a note the applicant will see) or Reject. The e-mail log shows whether each decision e-mail was delivered, with a resend option.

Directory when switched on. Review submitted profiles, tick the items you checked (identity, qualification, registration), set the date of the check and press Publish. Badges last twelve months; profiles due for re-verification are marked. Use Delist to remove a listing. Enquiry response times show how quickly practitioners answer, without the messages.

Crisis contacts when switched on. Enter one line per contact (name | phone | hours); they appear on every athlete and guardian screen. Check them regularly.

Record access (counts only) shows how many records each practitioner opened, never the content. Assessment licences records the licence for each published instrument. Account closures lists closures, with Restore during the cooling-off period and Purge erased records after it.

10. Athletes and guardians when switched on

Create an account. Open the athlete app, choose Create account, say whether you are an athlete or a guardian, enter your e-mail, name and password, accept the terms, then confirm your e-mail from the link we send. Then sign in.

Accept an invitation. Open the invitation e-mail, or sign in: invitations waiting for you are listed in your account. Read exactly what the practitioner will see and press I agree. Guardians tick the box confirming they are the athlete's parent or lawful guardian, type their full name as a signature and give the athlete's own e-mail address (it must be different from the guardian's); the athlete then gets their own invitation. Invitation and confirmation links work once: after you use them they are removed from the address bar and cannot be used again.

Check-ins and tasks. Answer each check-in (0 = not at all, 10 = extremely), tick I would like my practitioner to contact me urgently if you want to be contacted soon (this is not an emergency service), and press Mark done on tasks. Summaries your practitioner releases appear on your screen.

Your guardian sees your participation and released summaries. Press Let my guardian see my answers only if you want them to see your answers too (your practitioner must also allow it).

Approve a report for your coach. If your practitioner asks, you see exactly what the coach would see. Press Approve (optionally entering the coach's e-mail so we send the link) or Decline. You can Withdraw an approved link at any time.

Find a practitioner yourself. Signed in, use Find a sports psychologist, choose a practitioner and send a short enquiry; only the enquiry is shared until you accept an invitation. Use Switch practitioner to move to someone else; your current link ends when the new enquiry is sent.

Your account. Download my data at any time. Switch my account off (you can switch it back on from the sign-in page by e-mail), or Delete my account by typing DELETE and your password. Deleting ends every link and cannot be undone. End link stops sharing with one practitioner.

11. Finding a sports psychologist

The home page lists approved practitioners; choose one to send a service enquiry. when switched on The Find a Sports Psychologist directory lets you filter by sport, level, age group, language, online or in person and city. Results are alphabetical; badges show what the platform owner verified and when. Press Send an enquiry, fill in your name, contact, role and sport and a short message without health details, and press Send. You receive an acknowledgement with crisis contacts, and the practitioner replies to you directly.

12. Coaches and clubs

You do not need an account. Open the link you were sent to read the report. Links expire and can be withdrawn; if a link no longer opens, ask the athlete or the practitioner for a new one. Keep reports confidential and do not forward them.

13. Android app

Install the SPPS app provided by the platform owner. It opens the same SPPS service as the website, so your account, records and links are the same. Links in e-mails can be opened in Chrome or in the app; both work. Sign out from the app on a shared phone.

14. Troubleshooting

A link says it has expired. Verification, invitation and report links are time-limited. Ask for a new one, or for invitations sign in to see your waiting invitations. “Too many requests”. Wait a few minutes; forms and sign-in are rate-limited to stop abuse. “Pending verification”. Your practitioner account has not yet been approved. My listing disappeared. Editing a published profile takes it offline until it is published again, and expired badges hide a listing until re-checked. An athlete cannot see a task. The link must be active (both sides accepted). Nothing happens when I press a button. Check your connection and refresh; drafts are kept.
