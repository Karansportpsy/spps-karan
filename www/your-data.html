Your data

Who can see what you record in SPPS, where it is kept, and what we never do with it.

Where your data is stored

Practitioner workspace records (athletes, consultation notes, assessments, reports) are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database operated for SPPS. The hosting provider and region are named in the Privacy Policy. Consultation notes and case formulation text are additionally encrypted field by field before they are written, so they are unreadable in a database copy without the separate encryption key.

Who can see it

  • You. Only the practitioner who holds an athlete record can open it. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not only by the screens.
  • The platform owner. The owner reviews practitioner applications and sees operational counts (for example, how many records were opened in a month). The owner cannot read your athletes, notes, assessments or reports.
  • Other practitioners. Never, unless you and your client explicitly share something through a feature that asks for their consent.
  • Athletes, guardians, coaches. Only what you choose to share with them, and only with the consent the sharing screen asks for.

The access log

Every time an athlete record is opened or changed, SPPS records who did it and when — never what the record says. You can see this log for each of your athletes.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or your clients' data.
  • We never use it for advertising.
  • We never use it to train artificial-intelligence models, ours or anyone else's.

Taking your data with you

You can download everything you hold as one ZIP file at any time from Profile & Privacy. If you close your account, you choose what happens to each athlete record first. The platform owner keeps only your professional details (name, qualifications, review history) and a short record that the account was closed — never your clients' records.

Questions

Contact details for privacy questions and requests are listed in the Privacy Policy.
