A. Crisis and Safety Policy

A.1 SPPS is not a crisis, emergency or round-the-clock service. No one watches messages, check-ins, alerts or enquiries in real time.

A.2 If anyone is in immediate danger, call 112 (national emergency number) or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. The free, 24-hour Tele-MANAS mental health helpline is 14416. The crisis contacts maintained by the platform owner are shown on every athlete and guardian screen, on the public directory and in every enquiry acknowledgement.

A.3 The “contact me urgently” option on a check-in and any alert rule a practitioner sets only flag the item for the practitioner's attention the next time they look. They do not call, message or page anyone.

A.4 Practitioners must tell every athlete and guardian how, and how quickly, they respond, and what to do in an emergency, and must follow their professional duty-to-protect and safeguarding obligations outside SPPS.

A.5 Enquiry forms refuse messages that appear to describe self-harm, medication or diagnoses and show crisis contacts instead, because an enquiry is not the right place for that information.

B. Children and Guardians Policy

B.1 A child is anyone under 18. A child must not create an athlete account on their own or send an enquiry on their own; an enquiry about a child must come from their parent or guardian, and a practitioner can link with a child only through the guardian-first invitation below.

B.2 Guardian first. For an athlete record marked under 18, the practitioner's invitation goes to the guardian. The guardian reads exactly what will be shared, confirms they are the parent or lawful guardian, types their full name as a signature and gives consent. Only then is the child invited, and the child accepts for themselves.

B.3 What a guardian sees. Whether the child is taking part (for example, how many check-ins and tasks were completed) and the summaries the practitioner chooses to release to the guardian. A guardian sees the child's own answers only if both the practitioner and the child switch that on, and either can switch it off again.

B.4 Reports. A report about a child can go to a coach or club only with the guardian's recorded consent or approval.

B.5 No tracking or advertising. Children's data is used only for the service the guardian consented to. There is no advertising, profiling or behavioural tracking.

B.6 A guardian can withdraw consent at any time by ending the link or deleting their account; the link then stops and nothing further is shared.

B.7 Practitioners working with children must follow child-safeguarding law and practice, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 where relevant, and their professional code.

C. Practitioner Code of Conduct

Every practitioner using SPPS agrees to:

C.1 describe their qualifications, registrations and experience truthfully and work only within their competence and any licence the law requires;

C.2 obtain and record informed consent before recording information about an athlete, and keep that information confidential;

C.3 write their own clinical notes, formulations and reports, and sign only what they have written and checked;

C.4 use published assessment instruments only with the right licence, and never type copyrighted item wording into SPPS;

C.5 collect only what they need, share only with consent or legal authority, and use the sharing controls (audience-tagged report fields, clinical-only fields, consent records, expiring links) as intended;

C.6 answer directory enquiries promptly and courteously, including to say they cannot help, and never use enquiry details for marketing;

C.7 keep their account secure and not share sign-in details with anyone, including assistants;

C.8 refer on when an athlete needs care outside their competence, including medical or psychiatric care;

C.9 keep their own records of fees and agreements outside SPPS; and

C.10 close their account through the closure process, dealing with each client record responsibly.

A breach of this code may lead to delisting, suspension or closure of the account, and may be reported to the practitioner's registering or professional body where the law or safety requires it.

D. Directory Listing and Verification Policy

D.1 Opt-in. Only approved practitioners who submit a directory profile can be listed, and only after the platform owner publishes it. Approval of an account and publication in the directory are separate decisions.

D.2 What is shown. Only the fields in the published copy of the profile: display name, qualifications, sports, athlete levels, age groups, languages, online or in-person, city, whether taking new clients, an “about” text, a photograph if provided and verification badges. Editing a published profile takes it offline until the platform owner publishes the new version.

D.3 Verification badges. A badge (identity, qualification or registration) means the platform owner checked that item on the date shown. Badges last twelve months; a listing whose badges have all expired is hidden until re-checked. A badge is not an endorsement or a guarantee of the quality of a service.

D.4 Order. Results are alphabetical, with filters only. There is no ranking, rating, review, sponsored placement or payment for position.

D.5 Delisting. A profile is removed automatically when the account is suspended, sent back for review or closed, and may be removed by the platform owner at any time, for example if information is inaccurate or a complaint is upheld. The practitioner is told the reason where it is safe and lawful to do so.

D.6 Enquiries. Enquiries are passed to the practitioner named. The platform owner sees only counts and response times, not the messages.

E. Report Sharing Policy

E.1 Each report is written for one audience (for example athlete, coach or medical). Fields a practitioner marks as clinical-only are never included in any shared report.

E.2 A report can be shared only after it is signed, and only (a) through a link the practitioner creates after recording the consent of the athlete or, for a child, the guardian, or (b) through a link the athlete or guardian approves in the athlete app after seeing exactly what the coach will see.

E.3 Links expire (at most after 30 days), can be withdrawn by the athlete, the guardian or the practitioner at any time, stop working when the consent is withdrawn or the link between athlete and practitioner ends, and every attempt to open them is logged. Links a practitioner creates also stop working while that practitioner's account is inactive or not approved by the platform owner. Closing a practitioner account ends every athlete-app link and every report link and approved share that account created; they do not return if the account is later restored.

E.4 Recipients must keep reports confidential, use them only to support the athlete and not forward or publish them.

F. Acceptable Use Policy

G. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

G.1 Practitioner accounts are kept while active. A practitioner can download all their data at any time from Profile & Privacy.

G.2 Client records are kept for as long as the practitioner keeps them and their legal and professional duties require. Practitioners are responsible for deciding retention for their clients, including longer periods for records about children.

G.3 Athlete and guardian accounts can be downloaded, switched off and deleted by the account holder at any time. Deletion immediately removes the account, sign-in details and sessions, every invitation sent to that address and the account's enquiry copies; every link is ended and the account is detached from it; reports the account had approved for coaches stop opening. Check-ins and answers already shared stay in the practitioner's record, because they form part of the practitioner's professional record.

G.4 Enquiries stay with the practitioner they were sent to as correspondence received.

G.5 Practitioner applications that are refused or withdrawn are deleted on request, or once no longer needed for the review record.

G.6 Security records (record-opening logs, audit events, e-mail delivery records and report-link view logs) contain no clinical content and are kept as long as needed to protect the Platform, investigate incidents and meet legal duties.

G.7 Backups are made by our hosting provider and are overwritten on its rolling schedule; deleted data disappears from backups when they are overwritten. Encrypted fields in backups cannot be read without keys held separately.

H. Account Closure Policy (practitioners)

H.1 A practitioner starts closure from Profile & Privacy and confirms with their password. A notice period (30 days unless the platform owner announces otherwise) begins, during which the practitioner tells their clients and chooses what happens to each athlete record: export and erase (the record is exported to the practitioner and then erased) or keep deactivated (the record is kept, inactive, to meet retention duties). Closure can be cancelled during the notice period.

H.2 Closure does not need the agreement of every client. When it completes, sign-in stops and the directory listing is removed.

H.3 The platform owner keeps an archive of the practitioner's own professional information (for example their profile and application), never client records. During a cooling-off period (14 days unless announced otherwise) the platform owner can restore the account at the practitioner's request; after it, records marked export and erase are permanently erased.

I. Cookie and Device Storage Policy

SPPS uses only strictly necessary storage on your device. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies.

I.1 Practitioner session cookie — keeps you signed in to the workspace; not readable by scripts; expires after 10 hours or when you sign out.

I.2 Athlete app session cookie — keeps athletes and guardians signed in; not readable by scripts; sent only to the athlete app's own addresses.

I.3 Authentication token — stored by our authentication provider's library in your browser while you are signed in as a practitioner.

I.4 Theme — remembers whether you chose the original, light or dark look.

I.5 Registration draft — a copy of an unfinished practitioner application, kept on your device for up to 24 hours so you do not lose it.

I.6 Offline note drafts — if you lose connection while writing, the draft is encrypted on your device until it is sent.

I.7 Athlete app security token — protects your actions against cross-site attacks; kept only while the browser tab is open.

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings; you will then need to sign in again.

J. Automated and AI Features Policy

J.1 SPPS never generates clinical narrative. Notes, formulations and reports are written by practitioners.

J.2 Scores, bands, trends, charts, reminders and alert flags are calculated by fixed rules the practitioner can see. They are aids to professional judgement, not diagnoses, and missing data is shown as missing, never estimated.

J.3 Any drafting-assistance feature is switched off for the whole Platform unless the platform owner turns it on, and then is used only by practitioners who opt in themselves. Anything it produces is labelled as a draft that the practitioner must check, edit and accept as their own. It is never used on enquiries, check-ins or athlete messages, and is never used to make decisions about anyone.
