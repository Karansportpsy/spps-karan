Privacy Policy

Version: v1.0-free · Effective from: 17 November 2026 · Read with the Terms of Use and the Platform Policies.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Sport Psychology Practitioner Suite (“SPPS”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, how it is protected, and the rights you have. It covers the SPPS website, the SPPS Android app, e-mails we send and links we create. SPPS is operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}} under the WinMindPerform name. We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as their provisions come into force.

1. Who is responsible for your data

We are the data fiduciary for the data needed to run SPPS itself: practitioner applications and accounts, the public directory, enquiries, athlete and guardian accounts, links and their consent records, coach-link records, security logs and support correspondence.

Your practitioner is the data fiduciary for your client record. When a practitioner records notes, formulations, assessment scores, monitoring entries, injury psychology check-ins or reports about an athlete, the practitioner decides why and how that information is used. We store and process it on the practitioner's behalf, only to provide SPPS to them, and we do not use it for any purpose of our own. Questions or requests about a client record should go to the practitioner first; we will help them respond.

Contact for privacy questions and the Grievance Officer: {{OPERATOR_NAME}}, {{CONTACT_LINK}}.

2. What we collect

Visitors. Pages you open, the time, your browser type, and your network (IP) address, which is used briefly to protect forms and sign-in from abuse. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Practitioner applicants and practitioners. Name, e-mail address, password (held in hashed form by our authentication provider), qualifications, years of experience, achievements, specialisms, profile photograph, the text of your application, any information or documents you give the platform owner when asked, review decisions and notes, and the history of your application. Once approved: your workspace settings, sign-in sessions, directory profile (if you create one: display name, qualifications, sports, athlete levels, age groups, languages, delivery mode, city, availability and an “about” text), verification badges and their dates, records of your data exports and any account closure.

Client records kept by practitioners. Athlete code and name, sport, group, age group, guardian name and e-mail where the athlete is under 18, how they were referred, consent records, cases, consultation notes, signatures and amendments, case formulations, monitoring scores, assessment scores, injury psychology check-ins, interventions, reports and report-sharing consents. This information may include data about mental health, which is sensitive personal data and is handled with the protections in section 7.

Athlete and guardian accounts (the athlete app). E-mail address, display name, role (athlete or guardian), password (stored only as a salted, slow hash), verification and sign-in records, the links you accept and their consent records (date, time and, for guardians, the name you type as your signature), your check-ins and answers, tasks you complete, enquiries you send from your account, and your decisions on report sharing.

Enquiries. Your name, e-mail or telephone number, whether you are an athlete, parent or coach, sport, preferences you choose and a short message. Please do not include health details.

Coaches and clubs who open a report link. We record the date and time each link is opened and whether it was shown, expired or withdrawn. We do not ask you to create an account.

Support. Anything you send us when you contact us.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

We use personal data only for these purposes, with your consent or for another lawful use the law allows:

(a) to create and run accounts, sign you in and keep sessions secure; (b) to review practitioner applications and verify qualifications; (c) to publish directory profiles that practitioners choose to submit and the platform owner approves; (d) to pass enquiries to the practitioner you chose and send you an acknowledgement with crisis contacts; (e) to provide the practitioner workspace and store client records for practitioners; (f) to run the athlete app: invitations, links, consent, check-ins, tasks, alerts, released summaries and report-sharing decisions; (g) to create, log and expire report links; (h) to send service e-mails such as verification, invitations, decisions, reminders and links (we do not send marketing e-mails); (i) to keep the Platform secure, prevent misuse, investigate incidents and keep audit records; (j) to answer questions, requests and grievances; and (k) to meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 9). Withdrawal does not affect what was done before, and we may keep what the law requires us to keep.

4. Children

A child (a person under 18) can use the athlete app only after a parent or lawful guardian has given consent through a practitioner's invitation. The guardian consents first, typing their name as a signature; the child is then invited and accepts for themselves. Guardians see participation and the summaries the practitioner releases, and see the child's own answers only if both the practitioner and the child allow it. We do not track or monitor children's behaviour for any purpose other than the service their guardian agreed to, we show no advertising and we do not profile children. See the Children and Guardians Policy in the Platform Policies.

5. Who can see your data

The practitioner you link with sees what the invitation and consent screen listed, and nothing else.

Guardians see what is described in section 4.

Coaches and clubs see a signed report only when it was shared with consent: the practitioner recorded the athlete's (or guardian's) consent, or the athlete (or the guardian, for a child) approved it in the athlete app. Reports for coaches are prepared from fields the practitioner marked for that audience; fields marked clinical-only are never included.

The public sees only published directory profiles and the practitioner listings the platform owner approves.

The platform owner sees practitioner applications, directory submissions, enquiry counts and response times, counts of record openings (never their content), and closure requests. The platform owner does not read client records.

Service providers who host and run SPPS for us, under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide their service: Supabase (database and authentication, including verification e-mails), Render (application hosting), our e-mail delivery provider (service e-mails), and jsDelivr (delivers the authentication software library to your browser, which reveals your network address to it).

Authorities, where the law requires it, for example a court order or a lawful request under the Information Technology Act, 2000. Where allowed, we will tell the person concerned.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

6. Where your data is stored

SPPS runs on cloud servers that may be located outside India (for example in Singapore). Data is transferred and stored there under the safeguards described in this policy and in our providers' terms. We will follow any restriction the Government of India places on transfers to particular countries.

7. How we protect your data

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS, and encrypted database connections). Consultation notes, case formulations and signed report copies are additionally encrypted before they are stored, with keys kept separately from the database. The database enforces row-level security, so each practitioner's connection can reach only that practitioner's records, and athlete and guardian accounts are kept in a separate area that practitioner and public code cannot use to reach client records. Every opening of an athlete record is logged. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Public forms are protected by rate limits and automated-abuse checks. Access to production systems is limited to the platform owner. No system is perfectly secure; see section 10 for what happens if something goes wrong.

8. How long we keep it

Accounts are kept while they are active. Athlete and guardian accounts you delete are removed from our live systems straight away (account, sign-in details, invitations to your address and your enquiry copies); information you had already shared with a practitioner stays in that practitioner's record. Client records are kept for as long as the practitioner keeps their account and for the period their legal and professional duties require; when a practitioner closes their account the records are exported, handed over or deleted through the closure process. Enquiries are kept by the practitioner they were sent to. Rejected practitioner applications are deleted on request or when no longer needed for the review record. Security and audit logs are kept for as long as needed to protect the Platform and meet legal duties. Backups made by our hosting provider are overwritten on its rolling schedule. Details are in the Data Retention and Deletion Policy in the Platform Policies.

9. Your rights

You may: (a) ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used, and who it has been shared with; (b) ask us to correct, complete or update it; (c) ask us to erase it where it is no longer needed or you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it; (d) withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it; (e) nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to; and (f) have a grievance addressed by us and, if you remain unsatisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India once the relevant provisions are in force.

Many of these you can do yourself: athletes and guardians can download, switch off or delete their account and end any link in the athlete app; practitioners can download all their data from Profile & Privacy and start an orderly account closure. Otherwise write to {{CONTACT_LINK}}. We may need to confirm your identity first. Requests about a client record will be passed to the practitioner responsible for it.

10. If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach happens we will contain it, investigate it, inform the people affected without undue delay with what happened, what it means and what they can do, and report it to the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) and the Data Protection Board of India as and when the law requires.

11. Cookies and storage on your device

We use only what is needed for the Platform to work: a session cookie for practitioners and one for the athlete app (both protected from scripts and sent only to SPPS), the authentication provider's sign-in token, your display theme, a short-lived copy of an unfinished registration form (deleted after 24 hours), an encrypted copy of unsent offline note drafts, and a security token for the athlete app that is kept only while the tab is open. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Details are in the Cookie and Device Storage Policy in the Platform Policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices or the law change. The version and effective date are shown at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the Platform or by e-mail before they take effect and, where required, we will ask for your consent again.
